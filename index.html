Profiles

Visuals

Signature Gradients

Smooth color gradients across Unitframes, Raid Frames, Nameplates, Castbars, Aurabars, and Damage Meters — powered by cached Lua color tables for maximum FPS in 40-player encounters.

Custom Glow

Custom Glow Engine

Replaces Blizzard's default spell procs across Action Bars, Pet Bar, Unitframes, and Nameplates with six styles: Pixel, Autocast, Border Pulse, Bling, Proc, and Blizzard, plus Dispel Glow (with Pixel fallback), Shaman totem colors, and 3-color animated gradients.

NEW Minimap

Rectangle Minimap & Button Bar

Switch your minimap to a crisp 16:9, 4:3, or 2:1 rectangle (or classic circle) with Questie pin masking. Gather and skin addon and Blizzard minimap icons into a movable Minimap Button Bar with mouseover fade and combat hiding.

Hold to Cast Fix

Fixes Blizzard's Press and Hold Casting on ElvUI ActionBars in Retail and Forever by routing keybinds to native ActionButton commands, with optional spell macro conversion, auto-upgrading ranks, and /eltruism holdtocast.

Quick Installation

Get up and running in less than two minutes.

1

Install ElvUI

Eltruism is an ElvUI plugin. Make sure you have the latest version of ElvUI installed in your World of Warcraft AddOns folder.

Download ElvUI ↗
2

Download Eltruism

Grab Eltruism through the CurseForge App, Wago App, or directly via GitHub Releases and extract it to your AddOns directory.

CurseForge ↗ Wago.io ↗
3

Run In-Game Installer

Log into World of Warcraft to launch the setup installer automatically, or type /eltruism anytime to configure modules, browse the Help tab, or run /eltruism newchar on alts.

/eltruism

Handy Chat Commands

/eltruism Opens configuration (setup for installer, help for full command list)
/eltruism newchar Runs Chat Setup, CVars, and Edit Mode layout on alts
/eltruism fader Toggles UnitFrame and ActionBar fading on or off
/eltruism holdtocast Toggles the Press and Hold Casting fix on ElvUI ActionBars (Retail & Forever)
/eltruism paging [1-10] Swaps ActionBar paging between Bar 1 and Bar 4, or sets a specific bar
/eltruism color Swaps UnitFrames between Dark and Light modes
/eltruism gradient Toggles Gradient mode across the interface
/eltruism transparent Quickly toggles UnitFrame transparent health mode
/eltruism performance Applies performance CVars and disables heavy functions for maximum combat FPS
/eltruism encountercheck Disables non-essential visual effects during boss encounters for extra FPS
/way <x> <y> [title] Sets a map waypoint with distance, ETA, and arrow navigation (/eway with TomTom)
!keys / /!keys Links your Mythic+ or Delve keystone in chat and auto-slots it at the pedestal
Show more visual & utility commands
/eltruism autoadjust Toggles Borders automatically adjusting layout spacing
/eltruism chat Swaps chat panels between Dark and Transparent modes
/eltruism background Toggles UnitFrame backgrounds between grey and black
/eltruism elvuiskin Toggles the Eltruism ElvUI frame skin texture
/eltruism detailshide Toggles hiding Details! out of combat
/eltruism performancecvars Applies performance-oriented game CVars only
/eltruism togglemodels Toggles 3D portrait and frame models on or off
/eltruism update / autoupdate Resets settings to Eltreum defaults or toggles automatic updates
/eltruismdebug on / off Disables other addons and enables Lua errors for troubleshooting
