What Eltruism Delivers

Engineered for competitive raiding, Mythic+, leveling, and visual perfection. Every feature is modular and can be toggled to your exact preference.

⚔️
Profiles

Dual Tailored Profiles

Hand-crafted DPS/Tank and dedicated Healer profiles designed specifically for 16:9 displays and verified across 1080p, 1440p, 4K, and Ultrawide monitors. Pixel-perfect unitframes, actionbars, and raid frames with zero overlapping elements.

🌈
Visuals

Signature Gradients

Silky smooth color gradients across Unitframes, Blizzard Raid Frames, Nameplates, and Aurabars. All color math is cached in Lua for maximum FPS even during heavy 40-player combat encounters.

🔊
Audio Alerts

TTS Cooldown Voice Alerts

Configurable Text-To-Speech voice announcements for offensive and defensive cooldowns, interrupt notifications, and low health/mana warnings without cluttering your screen with extra icons.

🛡️
Nameplates

Dynamic Threat Nameplates

Intelligent nameplate height scaling, integrated power bar autopositioning, color-coded threat borders, and prominent debuff highlights for effortless mob grouping and crowd control.

🎯
Cursor HUD

Cursor Cast & Cooldown Rings

Rotating HUD rings anchored directly to your cursor indicating global cooldown (GCD), spell cast progress, and ability cooldown timers right where your eyes focus.

✨
Actionbars

Actionbar Glows & Classic Totems

Tailor spell activation and proc glow animations with custom speeds and sizes. Includes dedicated tracker glows for Shaman totems in Classic Era realms.

🎨
Themes & Skins

Sleek Soft Shadows & UI Skins

Manually fine-tuned soft frame shadows, dark & light UI themes, custom class textures, role icons, and comprehensive restyling for character stats, inspect windows, and dialogs.

💎
Unitframes

Transparent Unitframe Health

Easily toggle sleek transparent unitframe health backgrounds via the in-game chat command /eltruism transparent for maximum battlefield and environmental visibility.

🧭
Navigation

Smart Waypoints & Travel ETA

Enhanced /way command support that calculates real-time travel distance, estimated arrival time (ETA), waypoint naming, and navigation arrow tracking.

⚡
Quality of Life

Instant Fast Loot & Wishlist

True instant auto-looting with zero loot window lag, movable LootText alerts, automated vendor junk scrap, and a personal loot wishlist tracker for raid and dungeon drops.

💬
Chat & Social

Chat Enhancements & !keys

Inline item icons inside chat links, smooth chat fade when inactive, and the instant !keys shortcut to post your Mythic+ dungeon keystone to group, guild, or raid.

🎵
Immersion

Combat & AFK Immersion

Triggers custom music playlists during intense combat encounters, relaxing music during AFK screens with 3D character models, and bespoke mail arrival sounds.

⏱️
Castbars

Unified Interrupt Castbars

Unified player, target, and focus castbars with dedicated interrupt protection shields, interrupt success color coding, and cast timer tags.

⚙️
Modularity

Multi-Expansion & 100% Modular

Actively maintained for Midnight, MoP, Wrath, Classic Era, and WoW Forever. Every single module can be independently toggled on or off via /eltruism.

Quick Installation

Get up and running in less than two minutes.

1

Install ElvUI

Eltruism is an ElvUI plugin. Make sure you have the latest version of ElvUI installed in your World of Warcraft AddOns folder.

Download ElvUI ↗
2

Download Eltruism

Grab Eltruism through the CurseForge App, Wago App, or directly via GitHub Releases and extract it to your AddOns directory.

CurseForge ↗ Wago.io ↗
3

Run In-Game Installer

Log into World of Warcraft. The friendly setup guide will launch automatically, or type /eltruism to select your profile and preferences.

/eltruism

Handy Chat Commands

/eltruism Opens the full configuration window and setup installer
/eltruism transparent Quickly toggles unitframe transparent health mode
/way <x> <y> [title] Creates a map waypoint with distance, ETA, and arrow navigation
!keys Posts your current Mythic+ keystone in party, guild, or raid chat
